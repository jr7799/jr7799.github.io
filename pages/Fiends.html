Fiends (2025)

Fiends is a reverse shmup game where you play as different characters exploring the wilderness. Encounter different monsters in each region and survive the night unlock new characters and abilities. This game was created as an associates capstone project during my time at the New England Institute of Technology. I was the lead programmer and designer for this project, and I worked with a team that consisted of another programmer and designer. The game includes assets bought and paid for through the Unity Asset Store or various bundles.
