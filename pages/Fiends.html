Fiends (2025)

Fiends is a reverse shmup where you play as different characters exploring the wilderness. Encounter different monsters in each region, survive the night, and unlock new characters and abilities.

This game was created as an Associate's capstone project during my time at the New England Institute of Technology. I served as lead programmer and designer, working alongside a team consisting of another programmer and designer. The game includes assets purchased through the Unity Asset Store and various bundles.
